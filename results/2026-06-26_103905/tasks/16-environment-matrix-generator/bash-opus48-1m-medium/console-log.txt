## Solution

- Treats every key under `matrix` except `include`/`exclude` as an axis.
- `fail-fast` defaults to GitHub's `true` (uses explicit `has()` so `false` survives jq's `//`).
- `max-parallel` emitted only when configured; `include`/`exclude` passed through (GitHub expands them at runtime).
