## Summary

Built a test-results aggregator with strict red/green TDD, plus a CI pipeline validated end-to-end through `act`.

**Solution (`aggregator.py`, stdlib only):**
- Parses **JUnit XML** (`parse_junit_xml`) and **JSON** (`parse_json`, with status-alias normalization)
- `parse_file` dispatches on extension; `parse_directory` gathers every result file as one matrix leg
- `aggregate` computes totals (passed/failed/skipped/duration, per-file + overall) and detects **flaky** tests (passed in some runs, failed in others)
- `generate_markdown` renders a GitHub-Actions-ready summary with an overall PASSED/FAILED verdict
- `main` CLI writes to stdout and `$GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY`, with graceful errors and an optional `--fail-on-failure`
