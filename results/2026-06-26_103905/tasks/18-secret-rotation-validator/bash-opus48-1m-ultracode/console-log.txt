### One notable debugging finding
The first 3 `act` runs failed with `forcePull=true` → Docker Hub auth error. Root cause: `export ACT_FLAGS=(...)` — **bash arrays can't be exported across bats's per-test process boundary**, so the flags arrived empty and act fell back to `--pull=true` on a local-only image. Fixed by passing flags inline and writing `--pull=false` into the workdir `.actrc`; the corrected harness passed on the next run. Every step's logic had been pre-validated via `docker run` to avoid wasting act-push runs on logic bugs.
