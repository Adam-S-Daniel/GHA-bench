### Retention policy semantics
1. **MaxAgeDays** — delete older than cutoff (relative to a reference date).
2. **KeepLatestN** — per group (by `Name`), keep N newest, delete the rest.
3. **MaxTotalSizeBytes** — if retained total still exceeds the cap, delete oldest-first until under it.

Each is independently disable-able (value `0`), reasons accumulate per artifact, and dry-run is supported.
