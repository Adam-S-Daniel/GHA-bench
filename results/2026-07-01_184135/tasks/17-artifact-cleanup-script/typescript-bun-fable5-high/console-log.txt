## What was built

A TypeScript/Bun artifact-cleanup tool that takes a mock artifact inventory (`id`, `name`, `sizeBytes`, `createdAt`, `workflowRunId`), applies three retention policies, and emits a deletion plan with a summary, in dry-run or execute mode.

**Policy semantics** (applied in order, all reasons recorded per artifact):
1. **max-age** — delete artifacts strictly older than `maxAgeDays` relative to an injectable `referenceDate` (deterministic tests, no real-clock flakiness)
2. **keep-latest-N per workflow** — within each `workflowRunId`, only the N newest survive (ties broken by id)
3. **max-total-size** — evict oldest survivors until the total fits the byte cap, so space freed by the other policies counts first

**Layout:** `src/planner.ts` (pure planning + validation), `src/executor.ts` (injectable `ArtifactDeleter`; dry-run never invokes it), `src/parse.ts` (JSON validation with specific error messages — unknown policy keys, bad dates, negative sizes, duplicate ids all rejected), `src/cli.ts` (`--artifacts`, `--config`, `--dry-run` safety override; exits 1 with `error: …` on bad input).
